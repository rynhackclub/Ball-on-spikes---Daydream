GAME NAME : BALL ON SPIKES
CREATOR: Mohamed rayyan M

HOW BALL ON SPIKES COMES UNDER THE THEME SACRIFICE ?

The player must sacrifice safety and patience, carefully planning each jump to succeed.

The player’s goal is to reach the end, but they can’t rush.

In a way, the player sacrifices speed, comfort, or shortcuts to avoid the spikes.

Often in these games, players die multiple times before succeeding.

This represents the sacrifice of time and repeated effort to achieve success.
